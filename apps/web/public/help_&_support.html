Help & Support

Browse the answers below for the questions we hear most, from newsletter sign-up to cookie preferences. Can't find what you need? Our Contact page gets you straight to a human.

Reading & the newsletter

How do I subscribe to the newsletter?

Enter your name and email address in the subscription form located in the footer of any page, then click "Subscribe." You'll get a confirmation and can unsubscribe anytime from the link in any email we send.

What topics does Sholynk cover?

AI, Web3, software development, cybersecurity, gaming and the wider world of emerging technologies, which are practically explained and analysed.

How often do you publish?

New articles go up regularly across all categories. Follow us on social or subscribe to the newsletter so you don't have to keep checking back.

Working with us

How can I submit an article or news tip?

Reach out via the Contact page with your article idea or news tip. We review every submission and follow up if it's a fit.

Can I advertise on Sholynk?

Yes, we offer advertising and sponsorship opportunities. Send us a message via the Contact page with details about your brand and goals.

Can I republish or quote a Sholynk article?

Short quotes with a link back to the original article are welcome. For full republication, please contact us first for permission.

Technical & privacy

How do I report a technical issue with the website?

Email us at sholynktech@gmail.com with a description of the issue, the page it happened on, and your browser/device if you can. Screenshots help a lot.

How do I manage my cookie preferences?

Use the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page to reopen the preferences panel at any time and change what you've allowed. See our Privacy Policy for the full breakdown of what each cookie does.

How is my data handled?

We collect the minimum needed to run the site and the newsletter — details are in our Privacy Policy. We don't sell your data.
