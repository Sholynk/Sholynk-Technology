Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At Sholynk, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, and how you can control it.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal information you provide when subscribing to our newsletter or using the contact form, such as your name and email address.
  • Usage data automatically collected when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, and pages visited.
  • Anonymous engagement data — an unlinkable, randomly generated identifier stored in your browser so our reactions and comments widgets can enforce one vote per reader. This identifier is not personal information and cannot be traced back to you.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our website and services.
  • Send you newsletters and updates if you have subscribed.
  • Analyze website usage to enhance user experience.
  • Respond to messages sent through the contact form.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to run the site and, if you allow it, to remember preferences and understand how the site is used. When you first visit, a banner lets you accept everything, decline everything optional, or choose exactly what to allow. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page.

Cookie categories used on this site
Category Purpose Your control
Essential Keeps core features working — page navigation, the mobile menu, and recording your cookie choice itself. Always on; the site can't function without these.
Functional Remembers on-site preferences, such as this banner's choice, so you're not asked again on every visit. Optional — toggle in Cookie Settings.
Analytics Would help us understand, in aggregate, which articles and pages are useful. Optional — off unless you enable it in Cookie Settings.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and services, subject to confidentiality agreements.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information. You can unsubscribe from our newsletters at any time by following the unsubscribe link in the emails, and you can withdraw cookie consent at any time via Cookie Settings.

Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Material changes will update the "last updated" date above.
