Who's behind this


Sholynk was founded by Oluwashola Busari, a graduate of Olabisi Onabanjo University, Ago-Iwoye, Nigeria, with a B.Sc. in Mass Communication. He works as a software engineer with a handful of years of experience in JavaScript and Python, and has spent time in digital marketing along the way. That combination, building software and explaining things clearly is the whole premise of this site. Technical depth, delivered in language that doesn't require a computer science degree to follow.
